# Design of The Game

## Story
You play a 6th grader. You are in the middle of the school field. You have
some really, really good candy in your pocket. The school bell rings, and
suddenly a hoard of kindergarteners get out of school and come after you
to steal your candy! All you have is your trusty Nerf baseball bat. You defend
yourself by bopping the kindergarteners on the head, and they run away crying.
But more, and more kindergarteners keep coming! Wave after wave! Trying to steal
your candy!

## Layout
Top down view. You are in the middle of the screen. 2D pixel art. Waves of enemies
that get harder and harder. You earn points for each enemy "killed". The "killed"
enemies just run off screen crying. In between waves of enemies, you get a chance
to spend point on making your Nerf Baseball Bat stronger. You can earn new moves,
like making the bat have a bigger attack distance. Attacking faster. That kind of thing.
